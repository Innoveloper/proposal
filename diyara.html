Artisan Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding our zero-alcohol formulations, brass vessels, and sillage.

01 What makes Diyara formulation zero-alcohol and 100% skin-safe?
+
Traditional liquid perfumes use up to 80% denatured ethanol to force projection, which causes skin dryness and rapid scent evaporation. Diyara binds raw botanical extraits within a nourishing matrix of organic jojoba wax and beeswax. This creates a skin-identical lipid barrier that traps fragrance molecules for intimate pulse point diffusion.
02 How do solid perfume balms achieve 12-hour continuous sillage?
+
Alcohol evaporates within 2 hours taking scent with it. Solid balms rely on your body's natural 37°C pulse-point warmth (wrists, neck, collarbones). As your skin warms throughout the day, micro-droplets of pure extrait are gently activated and projected over a 12 to 14-hour timeline.
03 How does the solid brass refill pod system work?
+
Every Diyara vessel is precision CNC milled from solid architectural brass or anodized aluminum designed for a lifetime of use. When your balm finishes, insert the included magnetic pin into the base hole to pop out the empty tin pod and click your new fragrance refill in place with a satisfying snap.
04 Is the Aero car diffuser compatible with all vehicle air vents?
+
Yes. The Aero diffuser features a 360-degree silicone-cushioned universal grip that securely grips horizontal, vertical, and circular slats without scratching delicate interior leather or trim.
05 What is your shipping, freight, and return policy?
+
We provide complimentary climate-controlled express shipping on all orders worldwide. Every order includes complimentary discovery sample vials so you can test the fragrance before unsealing the master vessel. Returns of unopened items are accepted within 30 days.
