Behind The Brand

Two Women. Two Generations.
One Shared Dream.

House of Vyora was born out of a desire to build a candle studio rooted in creativity, clean burning, and independence. Founded by a mother and daughter, Vyora combines modern candle designs with Indian artisanal heritage.

The Daughter

A fashion designer with over a decade of experience, bringing an eye for contemporary forms, delicate palettes, and luxury finishes.

Her Mother

A lifelong artist who creates with her hands, finding joy and extraordinary intricacy in the smallest handcrafted details.

Our vision extends beyond candles: we are dedicated to building a studio created by women, powered by women, generating sustainable livelihoods and financial confidence across local artisan communities.
